# Audio workspace

Approved references: ../outputs/design-v10/Minutes-audio-neutral-v10.png and ../outputs/design-v11/Minutes-recording-detail.png.

Matte charcoal canvas, slightly raised neutral panels, thin rims and 10–12 px corners. Chinese sans typography, generous readable rows. White main controls with dark glyphs; muted mint for audio progress, coral for capture, small data-driven tag accents. No colored background wash or decorative glow.

Library: circular playback entrance, title and metadata, compact decorative audio motif, date. Capture: centered title, tabular timer, measured live level history, pause/stop controls; sources and metadata below. Detail: title and waveform transport, transcript and summary/chat in separate panels.

The existing desktop playback waveform is a seeded visual motif, not extracted acoustic samples. Live input history uses actual recorder levels. Do not represent decorative waveform shape as audio analysis.

Settings retain their existing layout with compatible dark surfaces. Prefer keyboard-operable controls, accessible text contrast and responsive wrapping.
